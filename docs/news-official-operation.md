# Official-First Daily News

## Current Status

Collection and distribution run, but collected fresh official releases cannot
enter the shared article worker. Its lead predicate accepts only publisher
templates, while the historical VW/Firefly worker supports narrow launch/battery
layouts. Fresh Swedish EV announcements therefore remain unused even when the
official article itself is accessible. This is a routing gap, not AI rejection.

## Execution Plan

1. Admit explicitly configured official pressrooms as direct, one-source leads.
   Fetch the real release, check authority, published date, access policy and
   narrative evidence. Do not invent a second publisher or copy a feed summary.
2. Reuse the existing literal-evidence writer, independent reviewer, exact-source
   refresh, duplicate checks, durable reservations and transactional publication.
   The 150-250-word recipe, daily/monthly limits and one-article UTC cap stay.
3. Improve free Swedish metadata triage for European EV market developments;
   keep hybrids, leasing/price offers, thin/stale documents and unvalidated table
   context out of paid writing. Reserve official candidate slots so a blocked
   media queue cannot starve accessible originals. Limited metadata can trigger
   a free fetch, but never skips actual content relevance or quality checks.
4. Run offline, SQL, build and exact-main CI checks, then dispatch the existing
   serialized Actions news workflow. Verify actual receipts, publication and
   production distribution, not merely a green collection job.

## Boundaries

No canonical vehicle writes, new credentials, budget increases, new SQL migration
or resurrection of old rejected/private attempts. Official company reporting is
attributed company reporting, not an independent test or a confirmed Swedish
specification. Existing publisher-to-primary and independent-editorial-pair
paths retain their checks. Historical VW/Firefly proof files remain untouched.
